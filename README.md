# PVDF Tactile Sensors for Prosthetic Hands

**Status:** Completed · October–December 2025  
**Area:** Materials engineering, tactile sensing

A poster comparing tactile-sensing materials for prosthetic fingertips. It evaluates PVDF, PZT ceramic, capacitive e-textile, and PDMS/carbon-black composite against flexibility, sensitivity, biocompatibility, and fatigue resistance.

The poster selects PVDF as the best overall fit for its stated fingertip requirements. Its comparison is a materials-selection study, not a prototype validation.
